# Bentosaur side walker — character source v002

**Status:** Gate 01 silhouette approved by the founder on 2026-08-02

This is the approved full-body, screen-right side-view identity for the first
Bentosaur locomotion pipeline. It replaces `char-v001` as the visual source of
truth. Approval covers the assembled character's identity, silhouette, palette,
and proportions only.

## Approved source

- `gate01/bentosaur_walker_side_gate01_chroma_v002.png` — immutable generated
  source on chroma magenta.
- `gate01/bentosaur_walker_side_gate01_transparent_v002.png` — transparent
  production reference derived locally from the chroma source.
- `gate01/bentosaur_walker_side_gate01_approval_board_v002.png` — the exact
  close-up/background-scale comparison that was approved.

## Locked visual contract

- oversized round head and frill;
- compact bean-shaped torso;
- tiny integrated arms with no visible elbow chain;
- extremely short, soft legs with no humanoid knee/shin chain;
- one continuous tapered tail;
- muted sage body, warm cream horns/belly, rounded dark outlines, and subtle
  storybook paper texture;
- no clothes, props, accessories, or independently regenerated body parts.

## Not approved yet

- layer separation or hidden overlap reconstruction;
- bones, meshes, pivots, weights, or constraints;
- idle, walk, expression, or transition animation;
- a Godot runtime integration strategy.

Before any body part is separated, the external 2D animation-tool research must
choose the authoring/runtime pipeline. The first technical acceptance gate after
that choice is a rest-pose reassembly that is visually indistinguishable from
the approved transparent source.
