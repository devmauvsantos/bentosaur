## Flat-cel gameplay v2

Reference 1:

`art/concepts/3d-diorama/bentosaur-gameplay-3d-diorama-engine-target-v2.png`

```text
Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: 9:16 vertical mobile game gameplay screen, explicit flat-2D
comparison candidate

Input images: Image 1 is the exact complete gameplay composition, screen
hierarchy, and layer order authority. Image 2 is only the chibi character
proportions, rounded silhouette language, sage/cream/coral palette, cozy
amber-versus-indigo lighting concept, and rainy-stall emotional reference. Do
not reproduce Image 2's 3D rendering.

Primary request: Create an unmistakably hand-drawn 2D Bentosaur service-loop
screen as a clean comparison against a dimensional diorama. Preserve the
lovable chibi design and complete gameplay loop, but translate every element
into animation-ready flat painted shapes.

Style/medium: premium 2D cel-painted game art with hand-inked dark-brown
outlines and a little digital-gouache grain. Every character, prop, building,
food item and UI element must be constructed from broad flat color regions
with one hard-edged shadow shape and one small highlight shape. Use clear line
art, simplified silhouettes, graphic rain streaks, hand-painted puddle shapes,
and layered paper-theatre depth. No ambient occlusion, no bevel shading, no
rounded 3D volume gradients, no specular plastic or clay surfaces, no PBR, no
ray-traced light, no tilt shift, and no photographic depth of field. It should
look like a beautifully animated 2D storybook game frame, not a render or a
filter.

Scene: cozy wooden bento stall at rainy blue-hour night, warm flat amber
lantern pools against cool indigo street planes, separate upright biped
dinosaur walkers in simplified background layers, steam and plants as looping
2D effects.

Character: same original sage-green baby Triceratops identity and proportions
as Image 2—huge round frill and head, tiny chubby biped body, cream
belly/horns/frill knobs/claws, coral cheeks, dark oval eyes, wide delighted
open smile, paws to cheeks—but redrawn completely as clean 2D line-and-color
animation art. No clothes or attached prop.

Required gameplay hierarchy: safe-area coin counter top left showing “00”;
three-star progress track centered; mood portrait top right; cream order bubble
containing exactly rice, pink berry and green leaf icons; large empty red
three-compartment bento in lower middle; exactly four bottom ingredient
bins—rice, berries, greens, tofu—with berry selected by a simple golden
outline. Large drag paths and touch targets. Preserve all major screen regions
and framing from Image 1.

Composition: fixed frontal 9:16 screen with a slight top-down food view. Clean
separable planes: far street, walkers, stall opening, customer, counter props,
bento, ingredient bins, HUD.

Constraints: original Bentosaur IP only. Unmistakably flat 2D drawing. No 3D
render, faux 3D, clay, plastic, photorealism, pixel art, anime screenshot
style, generic corporate vector art, franchise elements, logos, watermark,
gibberish text, extra menus, extra limbs, clothes, umbrella, tray, food, or
bento fused to a dinosaur. No words; only “00” may appear.
```

No additional flat-cel screens should be generated until the one-character rig
