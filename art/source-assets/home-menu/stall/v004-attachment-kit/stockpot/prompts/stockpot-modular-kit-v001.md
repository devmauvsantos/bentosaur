# Stockpot Modular Kit V001 Prompt

## Mode

Built-in image generation, reference-guided asset creation.

## Reference authority

`art/concepts/2d-chibi/v4/01_menu-refinement/bentosaur-home-menu-refined-classic-guestbook-v2.png`

Use the small dark cooking pot on the left side of the approved stall counter as
the design authority. Preserve its charming rounded graphite-metal form, warm
storybook rendering, restrained highlights, dark refined outline, and the
scene's slightly top-down front perspective.

## Generation prompt

Create one high-resolution modular 2D game-asset sheet for the Bentosaur stall
stockpot. Premium flat-cel storybook illustration, rounded cute proportions,
refined dark outlines, subtle hand-painted grain, warm low-contrast highlights,
consistent with the supplied approved Bentosaur home-menu reference.

Show exactly these isolated components, separated by very large empty gaps:

1. STOCKPOT BODY WITHOUT LID: a squat, rounded dark graphite/bronze cooking pot,
   front-facing with the same slight top-down view as the reference. Its open
   rim must be fully finished and believable, with a dark interior and a clean
   elliptical lip, so it still looks complete when the lid lifts away. Include
   the two small side handles as part of the body. No steam and no contents.
2. SEPARATE LID: the matching domed metal lid with its little center knob,
   shown completely detached and unobstructed, at the identical perspective and
   width needed to close the pot. The lid must be usable as a rigid 2D sprite
   rotating slightly around a bottom-edge pivot; do not merge it with the body.
3. SEPARATE SOFT CONTACT SHADOW: a restrained translucent-looking oval shadow
   shape for beneath the pot, shown as its own isolated element.
4. ASSEMBLED LOOK PREVIEW: one closed-pot preview showing how the exact body and
   lid should align. This preview is for visual guidance only; it must not add
   any new geometry or design details.

Use a perfectly flat solid #FF00FF chroma-key background with no texture,
gradient, vignette, or cast shadow outside the isolated contact-shadow element.
No dinosaur, no character, no stall, no scenery, no rain, no smoke, no steam,
no fire, no food, no lantern, no lettering, no labels, no watermark. Do not
crop or overlap any component. Keep every silhouette clean and production-ready.

Most important technical constraints: the body and lid are genuinely separate;
the lid exactly matches the body's rim; the lid does not contain any piece of
the body; the body has a complete open rim/interior hidden by the lid in the
assembled preview; all views share exactly one consistent perspective, palette,
outline, and light direction.
