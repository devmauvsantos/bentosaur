## Design Context

### Users

Bentosaur is for mobile players who love cozy, characterful games and return for short, comforting sessions. The home village should immediately communicate warmth, safety, and a living neighborhood before the player opens the stall.

### Brand Personality

Handmade, gentle, alive.

The game is cute and playful without becoming childish, noisy, generic, or disposable. Every visual effect should feel authored and emotionally connected to the tiny dinosaur community.

### Aesthetic Direction

- Premium flat-cel 2D storybook art with rounded forms, refined dark outlines, and softly painted texture.
- A traditional Japanese village framed by cool indigo night and restrained warm amber practical lights.
- Weather and seasonal motion should deepen coziness and place, not cover the art or become a spectacle.
- Avoid generic mobile-game gloss, visual clutter, photorealistic effects, harsh neon, exaggerated bloom, and obvious AI artifacts.
- Godot 4.7.x, portrait mobile presentation, and the Mobile renderer define the production envelope.

### Design Principles

1. Protect the approved illustration: runtime effects layer over immutable registered art.
2. Make the street feel inhabited through restrained environmental motion and asynchronous rhythms.
3. Preserve the warm-light versus cool-night composition in every weather state.
4. Prefer one memorable atmospheric gesture over many competing effects.
5. Target smooth mobile performance and provide reduced-motion and reduced-weather alternatives.
